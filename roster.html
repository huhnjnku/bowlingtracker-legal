Managing Your Roster

The roster is the pool of players available for match scoring. Access it from Settings → Roster (people icon in the top-right toolbar).

Adding a Player

  1. Open the roster manager.
  2. Use the segmented picker at the top to select the Boys Pool or Girls Pool. Players belong to one pool at a time.
  3. Type the player's name into the Add New Player… field.
  4. Optionally add a graduation year (4-digit, e.g. 2029).
  5. Tap the + icon to save.

The player appears immediately in the Active Pool list, sorted alphabetically.

Editing a Player

Tap a player's row to load them into the input row. Update their name or graduation year, then tap the checkmark icon to save.

Squad Assignment (Optional)

When editing a player, a chip row labeled SQUAD ASSIGNMENT (optional) appears. It lists up to six squad presets (Boys Varsity, Girls JV, etc.).

  • Empty (no chips selected) — the player appears in every squad's roster picker. This is the default and works for most bowlers.
  • One or more chips selected — the player only appears in the picker for those specific squads.

Only chips matching the player's gender show up (a girls-pool player won't see boys squads).

Assignment is a filter, not a gate. An assigned player is still on the roster — the assignment just controls which squad picker they show up in. It's most useful when you have overlapping rosters (e.g. a bowler who bowls Varsity and JV) or when you want to tidy up long picker lists.

Soft Delete (Remove)

Swipe left on an active player → tap Remove. This soft-deletes them: they disappear from the active roster and picker menus, but their match history is preserved.

Removed players show up under Graduated Bowlers at the bottom of the list.

Re-activating a Player

Swipe right on a graduated bowler → tap Re-activate. They rejoin the active pool immediately.

Permanently Deleting

Swipe left on a graduated bowler → tap Delete. This is permanent. Match history that referenced this player becomes orphaned (the score is preserved but the name shows as blank or the player ID).

Don't permanently delete a player who has real match history. Soft-delete instead — that keeps them out of the picker without breaking historical stats.

Merging Duplicates

If you accidentally create two records for the same person (e.g. Sam Meyer and Sam Myers), use manual merge:

  1. Swipe right on the incorrect version → tap Merge Into… (orange).
  2. Pick the canonical version from the destination list. Only same-pool candidates appear.
  3. Confirm the merge dialog.

The source player's match history gets rewritten onto the destination player, and the source is permanently deleted.

Merge is destructive and can't be undone. Double-check you picked the right destination.

Auto-Repair (Toolbar Wand)

The Repair button (wand icon, top-left toolbar) runs an automatic deduplication pass. It scans for orphaned duplicates from older CSV imports and consolidates them. Safe to run any time — if there's nothing to fix, it just tells you so.

Graduation Year

The graduation year is used for filtering in the Statistics dashboard (e.g. "Class of 2028 only"). It's optional and can be added/edited at any time.

Sharing the Roster with Parents

Tap the QR icon at the top of the roster manager to open the share screen. This generates a QR code parents can scan in their copy of the app. See Sharing & Export for details.

Roster Version

Every time you add, edit, or remove a player, the roster version increments. Parents who have an older version see a warning that their tracked-match player may no longer be on the current roster.
