BowlingTracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-07

BowlingTracker is a coaching tool for high school bowling coaches, parents, and their teams. This policy explains what information the app handles, how it's stored, and how it's shared.

What information is entered into the app

You (the coach or parent) enter all information manually. The app does not collect any information automatically. Specifically, the app can store:

  • Roster information you enter for your team: player name, gender designation, graduation year, and an active/inactive flag.
  • Opponent lineup names you type in during match setup.
  • Match scores and pin-by-pin telemetry you record during practices and matches.
  • School name and brand colors you set during onboarding.

No account, sign-in, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, health data, or advertising identifier is collected.

Where the information lives

Everything you enter is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. The information is included in your device's normal iCloud/iTunes backup if you have those enabled — that backup is managed by Apple, not by BowlingTracker.

BowlingTracker has no servers. We do not receive, store, or process your data on our own infrastructure.

When information leaves your device

BowlingTracker only shares data when you explicitly trigger a share, using the standard iOS share sheet:

  • AirDrop, Messages, Mail, Files.app — for coach-to-coach match handoff (.bowlingmatch, .bowlingmatchjson, .bowlingseasonjson files).
  • QR codes you generate to share your roster with a parent's device. The parent's device stores a read-only copy of the roster you shared.
  • Parent packet exports for parent-to-coach handoff of a bowler's practice data.
  • PDF and CSV reports you choose to export.

We do not transmit anything in the background. Every share is initiated by you.

Camera permission

BowlingTracker may request access to your device's camera for its Lane Camera feature (used to record a shot for ball-tracking analytics). Camera access is used only while the Lane Camera tool is actively running on-screen. Video and derived tracking data stay on your device — nothing is uploaded. You can revoke camera access at any time in Settings → BowlingTracker → Camera.

Third-party services, analytics, and advertising

BowlingTracker does not use third-party analytics SDKs, crash reporters, advertising networks, or tracking tools. There are no in-app ads.

Minors

Coaches use BowlingTracker to record information about high school student-athletes, who may be minors. The app is designed for use by adults (coaches, parents) who have a legitimate interest in tracking that information (roster and performance data for their team or child).

  • We recommend coaches obtain permission from their school and/or the athlete's parents before entering personal information.
  • BowlingTracker does not knowingly collect information directly from a minor. Athletes are not intended app users.

If you believe a minor's information has been improperly entered into BowlingTracker on a device you control, you can delete it via the in-app roster management screens or by uninstalling the app.

How to delete your information

  • In-app: use the roster and match management screens to delete individual records.
  • Uninstall the app: iOS will remove all local BowlingTracker data along with the app.
  • Revoke a shared roster: parents can delete a scanned roster snapshot in the parent-side Rosters screen at any time.

Because we don't host your data on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete on our end.

Changes to this policy

If we change how BowlingTracker handles data, we will update this policy and update the "Last updated" date above.
