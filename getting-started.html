Getting Started

First Launch

The first time you open Bowling Tracker, you'll see a mode picker with two big buttons:

  • Coach — "Track full matches, manage your roster, log scores and statistics."
  • Parent — "Track pin telemetry for your bowler and share it back to their coach."

Pick whichever fits. You can switch later in Settings — nothing here is permanent.

Coach Onboarding

If you chose Coach, you'll be asked to name your school. This appears in exports, PDFs, and (optionally) in shared match snapshots. Enter something like Batavia High School and tap Get Started.

That's it — you'll land on the main Coach interface with four tabs:

  1. Log Match — score a match live, one squad or several at once
  2. Historical Matches — view, edit, share, and export past matches
  3. Statistics — season averages, per-player breakdowns, W-L records
  4. Settings — profile, colors, roster, format toggles, Live Match Scoring

Parent Onboarding

If you chose Parent, you'll see a confirmation screen and tap Enter Parent Mode. No setup required. Parent mode has four tabs:

  1. Track — track pin data for your bowler during a match
  2. Live — follow live scores from your coach (Live Match Scoring subscribers)
  3. Statistics — stats for bowlers you've tracked
  4. Settings — colors, mode switch

To do anything useful in Parent mode you first need a coach roster. The coach shares one via a QR code — see Parent Mode for the scan flow.

Switching Modes Later

Go to Settings → scroll to App Mode → tap Switch to Parent or Switch to Coach. A confirmation dialog warns you what will change.

Your data is preserved across mode switches. Coach-mode matches don't disappear when you jump into Parent mode — they're just hidden from that view. Switch back and they're all there.

Adding Your First Players

Before scoring your first match, you'll want a roster. Head to Settings → Roster (person icon in the toolbar) — that's the roster manager. See Managing Your Roster for the full walkthrough.

What Data Lives Where

By default, everything lives locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData. Nothing is sent to any developer server. If you enable Live Match Scoring, data syncs through your own iCloud account — the developer never sees it.

Privacy note: The app uses no analytics, no ad networks, and no third-party tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.
