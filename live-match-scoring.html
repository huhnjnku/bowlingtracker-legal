Live Match Scoring

Live Match Scoring (LMS) is the app's one paid feature. A single $4.99 in-app purchase unlocks it forever on any device signed into the same Apple ID.

What LMS Actually Does

LMS is an iCloud-backed sync layer bolted on top of the app's normal scoring flow. It uses Apple's CloudKit, so your data flows through your own iCloud account — not any third-party server.

  1. Coach-to-coach sync — score varsity and JV on separate devices, everyone stays in sync.
  2. Parent live scoring — parents follow the match from anywhere in real time.
  3. Push notifications — parents get pinged when the match starts, when it ends, and on any 279+ game.
  4. Google Sheets bridge — optional live push to a spreadsheet for public broadcasts. See Google Sheets.

Three Sync States

Every squad has one of three sync states, indicated by pills in Settings:

StatePillMeaning
Local Only (no pill) The squad exists on this device only. Matches you score stay local. Nothing pushes to iCloud.
Synced Synced (green cloud icon) The squad is published to iCloud. Matches you save are automatically pushed. Other devices signed into your Apple ID can see it, but no one else can.
Shared Synced + Shared (people icon) You've generated an invite URL. Anyone you send that URL to (co-coaches, parents) can subscribe.

Setting Up LMS from Scratch

Step 1 — Turn on LMS

Settings → toggle Use Live Match Scoring. First tap opens the paywall (unless you're grandfathered). Purchase confirms via StoreKit; the toggle then flips on.

Step 2 — Own or Join?

If you'll be the primary coach creating squads for your program, keep I create and share squads on. If another coach owns the squads and will invite you, flip it off.

Step 3 — Create a Squad (Owner Path)

  1. Tap Add Squad.
  2. Enter the squad name (e.g. Boys Varsity).
  3. Pick the pool (Boys or Girls).
  4. Tap Save.

The squad is now Local Only. It shows up in the Log Match tab's Add Squad menu.

Step 4 — Publish

In the squad row, tap Publish. This creates the squad record in iCloud and enables sharing. Status flips to Synced.

Step 5 — Share

Tap the Share icon in the squad row. iOS opens the CloudKit share sheet. Tap Copy Link, then send it to parents and co-coaches via text, email, or whatever channel you use.

Step 6 — Backfill History (Optional)

When you first publish, the app offers to backfill the current season's matches. Say yes and every existing match for that squad gets pushed so co-coaches see the historical data too.

You can push more history later with the Push History button — it does a one-shot publish of every match for the squad, all seasons, showing a toast summary when done.

Joining a Squad Someone Else Owns

Two paths:

Path A — Tap the invite URL

If iOS lets you tap the URL, it opens the app and pops a "You've been added to {squadName} as a co-coach" alert. Done — the squad appears in Squads You Co-Coach.

Path B — Paste the URL

If iOS shows a "you need a newer version" error (common pre-launch), use Join via URL in Settings. Paste the URL manually. Same result.

Paste-URL fallback exists for a reason. Before an app is live on the App Store, iOS can't verify the receiving app. Once the coach app is publicly available, tap-to-accept starts working — and the paste-URL button auto-restores state on next launch.

How Publishing Works During a Match

While you're scoring a match with LMS on:

  1. Every score change triggers a debounced publish (2-second delay).
  2. The snapshot goes up to iCloud.
  3. Co-coaches and parents see the update within seconds.

The debounce means rapid typing (frame-by-frame corrections) coalesces into one publish, not one per keystroke. Saves battery and network.

Match Started / Final Notifications

Parents subscribed to your squad get:

  • Match Started — fires when you enter your first score. Wording: "Boys Varsity just started their match vs Central High, follow live scoring in the Bowling Match Tracker App."
  • Match Complete — fires when you tap Save. Wording: "Final: Batavia 2500, Central 2350" (adjusts to include all opponents for tri/quad).
  • Individual 279+ — fires the first time a player hits 279 or higher in a game. Example: "Alice just bowled a 300!"
  • Baker 279+ — same idea for baker games. "The team just bowled a 285 Baker game!"
Notifications never fire for Open Tracking matches. Open Tracking is designed for practice and doesn't publish anywhere.

Editing Shared Matches

If you edit a match that was previously published, the update republishes on save. Co-coaches see it the next time their app refreshes. Deletions publish a tombstone so co-coaches see the match disappear.

Co-Coach Zone Routing

When a co-coach scores a match for a shared squad, their save publishes to the owner's iCloud zone — not the co-coach's own private one. This is invisible to you as a user, but it's why the ownership toggle matters: it tells the app whether to expect this squad's data in your zone or someone else's.

What Happens if You Turn LMS Off

  • Local matches stay local. No changes to your on-device data.
  • Published squad records stay in iCloud — the app just stops publishing new updates.
  • Co-coaches and parents can still see historical data you already published.
  • If you turn LMS back on later, the squads reappear and publishing resumes.

Grandfather Rule

The first time you launch the paid version of the app, if you already had a CoachSquad from a pre-launch build, LMS unlocks automatically for free. You never see the paywall. This is one-way and permanent — even if you refund a purchase later, grandfather status persists.

Restore Purchases

If you install the app on a new device (same Apple ID), open the paywall → tap Restore Purchases. Your prior purchase applies to the new device.
