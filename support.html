Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Your rosters, match scores, and telemetry are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Nothing is sent to the developer's servers.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings inside the app, tap the "Use Live Match Scoring" toggle, and choose "Restore Purchases" in the paywall. Restores are handled by Apple against your Apple ID.

Can parents follow the scores?

Yes — with the optional Live Match Scoring purchase, you can share a link with parents so they can follow the match live from their own device.

How do I share a roster with parents?

In the Roster tab, tap the QR-share icon. Parents scan the QR code in their copy of the app to pull the roster onto their device. No accounts or servers required.

Response time

This is a one-person project. Expect a response within a few days during bowling season, longer in the off-season.
