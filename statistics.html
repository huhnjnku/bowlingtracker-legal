Statistics Dashboards

The Statistics tab summarizes team and per-player performance. All stats are derived from your saved matches — there's no separate stats database to maintain.

Top Filters

Every stat on this page is scoped by the filters at the top. Change any of them and the numbers below update immediately.

  • Season — dropdown of school years derived from your history. Uses the Aug 1 – Jul 31 boundary.
  • Gender pool — Boys or Girls.
  • Squad — All Squads, or a specific one (e.g. Boys Varsity).
  • Format — All Formats, H2H Only, or Tourney Only.
  • League — All Matches or League Only.

Team Metrics Cards

Four cards summarize the whole team under the current filter:

  • Ind Avg — team-wide individual average across all games in scope.
  • Baker Avg — team-wide baker average.
  • Record — W-L (H2H format) or High Match (Tournament format).
  • Include Multi — a small checkbox that only appears if any tri/quad matches are in scope. When on, tri/quad matches contribute pairwise W-L; when off (default), they're excluded from the record calculation.
Why exclude tri/quad from W-L by default? A tri or quad match doesn't cleanly map to a single win or loss — you have three or four possible outcomes. The default keeps your record straightforward, and you can opt-in via the Include Multi toggle if you want the full picture.

Graduation Class Filter

Below the metric cards, a picker lets you narrow the player list:

  • Active Roster — only players currently marked active.
  • All Graduated — historical bowlers.
  • Class of YYYY — a specific graduating class.

Player Cards

Below the filter, each player in scope gets a summary card:

  • Name and pool badge
  • High game
  • Average
  • Strike percentage
  • Spare percentage
  • Match count

Tap any card to open the Individual Stats Detail view.

Individual Stats Detail

The per-player view breaks down that bowler's performance:

  • All top-level metrics (average, high game, strike/spare rates)
  • Season vs. career totals
  • Condition splits (House vs. Sport oil)
  • League vs. non-league breakdown
  • Baker pin telemetry (via read-time attribution — see below)
  • A performance timeline chart showing each game's score across the season

Baker vs. Individual Attribution

The app is careful about baker vs. individual games:

Baker pins never count toward per-player individual averages. A baker game rotates bowlers across frames, so it doesn't reflect any one bowler's performance. Individual averages only include games where a single bowler bowled the entire game.

Baker performance still shows up on individual detail cards — but in a separate section, clearly labeled.

How the Numbers Get Computed

All statistics are computed live from your MatchRecord rows and pin telemetry. There's no separate "stats" storage. This means:

  • Editing a match instantly updates statistics.
  • Deleting a match removes those games from stats.
  • Importing a match (or having one arrive via Live Match Scoring) includes it in stats immediately.

Which Pool Am I Looking At?

Every match is tagged with a pool (Boys or Girls). The Statistics dashboard filters strictly by that pool, so a girls-pool player never contaminates boys averages and vice versa.

If your Team Overview stats look off: the most common cause is stale squad-name data from an old build. The app auto-derives the pool from the squad name where possible ("Boys Varsity" → Boys), but if a squad name is ambiguous (e.g. just "Varsity"), it may not attribute correctly. Rename the squad to include the pool word.
